## Author contributions

The contribution statement follows the CRediT taxonomy and the development
lineage represented in this repository.

- **Elephes Sung:** Methodology, Software, Formal analysis, Visualization,
  Writing - original draft, Writing - review and editing. Developed the
  Bayesian inference framework and integrated the ABM and inference layers as
  a reusable Python package.
- **Szonja Skenderovic:** Methodology, Software, Validation, Investigation,
  Visualization, Writing - original draft, Writing - review and editing.
  Developed the refined Hookean interaction, binding, unbinding, and model
  validation framework.
- **Yixuan Li:** Conceptualization, Methodology, Software, Investigation,
  Validation, Writing - original draft. Developed the original killer-target
  agent-based model on which the refined implementation builds.
- **Ruben Perez-Carrasco:** Conceptualization, Methodology, Supervision,
  Project administration, Writing - review and editing. Conceived and
  supervised the research programme and guided model and inference
  development.
